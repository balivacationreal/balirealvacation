Who We Are

More Than Just a Tour – It's a Real Vacation

Welcome to Bali Real Vacation. We specialize in crafting premium, hassle-free travel itineraries across Bali and Nusa Penida for both domestic and international visitors.

We know firsthand that planning a trip for a large group—balancing the energy of teenagers with the comfort needed for parents, aunts, and uncles—can be overwhelming. That is why our team handles every detail, from fast boat tickets to experienced local drivers, ensuring a seamless journey whether you are traveling with your extended family, a partner, or solo.

Beyond providing unforgettable adventures, we are deeply committed to the islands we showcase. We advocate for sustainable, eco-conscious tourism to protect our beautiful marine life and raise awareness about critical local environments, such as the delicate coral reefs in North Bali. When you explore with us, you are supporting a vision that respects and preserves the true beauty of Indonesia.

Bali Travel Essentials

Everything you need to know before you touch down on the Island of the Gods.

☀️ Peak Season

Dry Season

Clear skies, sunny days, and ideal surf conditions. Perfect for island hopping to Nusa Penida and hiking Mount Batur.

Apr May - Sep Oct
🌧️ Low Season

Wet Season

Lush, green, and magical. Fewer crowds, lower prices, and waterfalls like Sekumpul are at peak power. Rain usually lasts only a few hours.

Nov - Mar

Visa on Arrival

Most nationalities can get a 30-day VOA at the airport or apply online beforehand.

Power Plugs

Bali uses European-style Type C and F two-pin plugs (230V).

Cultural Respect

Cover shoulders and knees at temples. A sarong is usually required and provided.
