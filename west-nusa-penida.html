West Nusa Penida Tour

Dramatic Cliffs, Pristine Beaches, and Hidden Treasures

Escape the ordinary and discover a hidden paradise with our exclusive Private Day Trip to West Nusa Penida Island. Prepare to be captivated by dramatic cliffs, pristine beaches, and secret treasures awaiting your arrival. We ensure your worries vanish the moment you step into your private transportation. This is much more than a simple day trip; it's your all-access pass to a truly unforgettable Nusa Penida adventure.

Tour Itinerary

  • 06:30 AM - Hotel Pick-up

    Private transfer from your Bali hotel to Sanur Harbor.

  • 07:30 AM - Fast Boat to Penida

    Hop on the high-speed boat for a scenic ride across the strait.

  • 09:00 AM - Angel's Billabong

    Explore the stunning natural infinity pool carved into the rock face.

  • 11:00 AM - Broken Beach

    Marvel at the spectacular circular cove and its natural rock archway.

  • 12:00 PM - Kelingking Beach

    Visit the iconic T-Rex shaped cliff and take breathtaking panoramic photos.

  • 01:00 PM - Lunch

    Enjoy a delicious local lunch featuring Balinese or Western foods.

  • 02:30 PM - Crystal Bay

    Relax, swim, and soak up the sun at this beautiful palm-fringed beach.

  • 04:30 PM - Return to Bali

    Fast boat ride back to Sanur Harbor and transfer back to your hotel.
