Nusa Penida Snorkeling & Land Tour

Swim with Manta Rays & Explore Iconic Cliffs

Experience the ultimate Nusa Penida adventure with our premium all-inclusive day trip. Dive into crystal-clear waters to swim alongside majestic Manta Rays, then explore the island's most famous land attractions. This comprehensive package combines three world-class snorkeling spots with the breathtaking cliffs of the West Coast — the best of both the ocean and the island in one unforgettable day.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable footwear for cliffside walking
  • Swimwear and a change of clothes
  • Reef-safe sunscreen (strongly recommended)
  • Motion sickness medication if needed

Tour Itinerary

  • 06:00 AM – Hotel Pick-up

    Pick-up at your Bali hotel and private transfer to Sanur Harbor.

  • 09:00 AM – Snorkeling Adventure

    Swim with Manta Rays at Manta Bay, Gamat Bay, and Crystal Bay.

  • 12:00 PM – Lunch

    Enjoy a delicious local lunch to refuel after snorkeling.

  • 01:30 PM – Angel's Billabong

    Visit the stunning natural infinity pool carved into the rock face.

  • 02:00 PM – Broken Beach

    Marvel at the spectacular circular cove and natural rock archway.

  • 03:00 PM – Kelingking Cliff

    Experience the breathtaking T-Rex cliff viewpoint and panoramic ocean views.

  • 04:45 PM – Return to Bali

    Fast boat ride back to Sanur Harbor, then private transfer to your hotel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Basic swimming ability is required. Life vests are provided for all guests and our experienced guides assist beginners throughout. If you cannot swim at all, we recommend our sightseeing-only tours instead.

Manta Ray sightings are highly likely at Manta Bay and Gamat Bay where these creatures feed regularly. We cannot guarantee wildlife sightings as they are wild animals, but sightings occur on approximately 90% of our tours.

We provide top-tier masks, fins, snorkels, rash guards, and life vests for all guests. Just bring your swimwear and reef-safe sunscreen — all equipment is included in your price.

The dry season (May to October) offers the best underwater visibility and calmest sea conditions. Manta Ray sightings are most frequent between May and October when ocean currents bring abundant food into the area.

We pick up guests at 06:00 AM to reach the snorkeling spots during the optimal early morning window, before afternoon winds and boat traffic arrive. Early mornings offer the best water clarity and the highest chance of spotting Manta Rays and sea turtles.
