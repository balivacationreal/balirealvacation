North Bali Canyoning

Adrenaline, Waterfalls, and Hidden Gorges

Experience the ultimate adrenaline rush deep in the hidden canyons of Gitgit and Sambangan. Rappel down thundering waterfalls, slide through sculpted volcanic rock gorges, and plunge into crystal-clear jungle pools. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced thrill-seeker, our certified local guides ensure an unforgettable, safe, and action-packed adventure.

Frequently Asked Questions

No experience is needed for our beginner and intermediate canyons (Egar and Maboya). Our certified guides provide a full safety briefing and hands-on training before you enter the canyon. The Egar Canyon is specifically designed for first-timers and families.

We provide a complete canyoning kit: wetsuit, harness, helmet, and canyoning shoes. We also capture free action photos and video footage of your descent that you receive after the tour — included at no extra charge.

Choose Egar Canyon (Beginner) if it's your first time — 6 rappels up to 15m, perfect for families. Maboya Canyon (Beginner+) has 9 rappels to 21m with 2 optional jumps. Samba Canyon (Intermediate) has 11 rappels to 35m and requires swimming ability. Aling Canyon (Advanced) includes a 50m mega-rappel for experienced canyoners, dry season only.

The minimum age is 7 years for Egar Canyon and 12 years for Maboya and Samba Canyons. All participants under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian. There is no upper age limit for healthy adults.

Bring a swimsuit to wear under the provided wetsuit, a change of dry clothes, and a towel. Leave valuables at your hotel as the environment is fully wet. We provide all technical gear, a traditional Balinese lunch, and hot shower facilities after the tour.
