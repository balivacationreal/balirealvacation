Frequently Asked Questions

Fill in the booking form above or send us a WhatsApp message with your name, flight number, arrival date/time, and destination hotel. We will confirm your booking within minutes and send full pickup details.

The price covers a private A/C vehicle from Ngurah Rai Airport (DPS) directly to your hotel, a professional driver who monitors your flight arrival in real time, and complimentary cold drinks for each passenger.

Our driver monitors your flight status and adjusts pick-up accordingly. You will not be charged extra for flight delays. The driver will wait for you with a name sign in the arrivals hall — no stress on arrival.

Standard pricing covers up to 4 passengers with standard luggage. For groups of 5 or more, or guests with extra luggage, we can arrange a minivan at a slightly higher rate. Mention your group size when booking.

An airport transfer is a direct one-way journey between DPS Airport and your hotel. A private driver tour is a full-day sightseeing experience across Bali with an English-speaking driver-guide. Both can be booked through us.
