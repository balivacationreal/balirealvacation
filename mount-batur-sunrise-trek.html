Mount Batur Sunrise Trek

A Breathtaking Morning From an Active Volcano

Wake up before dawn for an unforgettable adventure under the stars. Mount Batur is one of Bali's most sacred and active volcanoes. Hiking to its 1,717-meter summit provides the most spectacular sunrise view on the island. Ascend the volcanic trails with our certified local guide, watch the sky turn brilliant shades of orange and pink over Lake Batur, then enjoy a truly unique breakfast cooked using natural volcanic steam.

Tour Itinerary

  • 01:30 AM – Hotel Pick-up

    Early private transfer from your hotel to Kintamani base camp.

  • 03:30 AM – Briefing & Gear Up

    Meet your certified local guide. Enjoy a hot coffee or tea and receive flashlights and trekking poles.

  • 04:00 AM – The Ascent

    Begin the hike up volcanic trails under the starry night sky. The summit takes approximately 2 hours.

  • 06:00 AM – Sunrise & Volcanic Breakfast

    Reach the summit in time for a glorious sunrise. Eggs and bananas cooked in volcanic steam vents.

  • 07:00 AM – Crater Exploration

    Walk the volcanic rim, photograph Mount Agung, and meet the friendly mountain monkeys.

  • 09:30 AM – Coffee Plantation (Optional)

    Stop at a traditional Balinese coffee plantation to sample the famous Luwak coffee.

  • 11:30 AM – Return to Hotel

    Private transfer back to your hotel to rest after your incredible morning.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Mount Batur trek is rated moderate difficulty. The trail is approximately 4–5 km with an elevation gain of around 700 meters over loose volcanic scoria. Most healthy adults of average fitness can complete it. The ascent takes about 2 hours and the descent around 1.5 hours.

Hotel pick-up starts at 01:30 AM to arrive at the trailhead by 03:30 AM. We begin the hike at 04:00 AM in darkness to reach the 1717-meter summit for the sunrise, which typically occurs between 06:00–06:30 AM.

We provide flashlights and trekking poles. You should bring: warm clothing and a windproof jacket (it is cold at 4 AM!), hiking shoes with good grip, plenty of water, sunscreen for after sunrise, and a camera. Light snacks for energy on the ascent are also recommended.

Yes, Mount Batur is one of Bali's most popular and regularly climbed volcanoes. Volcanic activity is closely monitored by Indonesian authorities and our certified local guides know all safety protocols. Climbing is suspended during volcanic alert periods.

At the summit, our guides cook eggs and bananas by placing them directly into the natural volcanic steam vents. You enjoy this unique breakfast while watching the sunrise over Lake Batur and Mount Agung — an experience you simply cannot find anywhere else in the world.
